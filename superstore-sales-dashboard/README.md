## 🎯 Objective
Designed a Power BI dashboard to analyze retail sales performance of a Superstore across the United States, helping stakeholders identify regional strengths, top-performing product categories, and seasonal revenue trends.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboard design, KPI cards, and visual storytelling
- **DAX** — Calculated measures for Sales, Profit, and Quantity KPIs
- **Excel/CSV** — Data source (Sample Superstore dataset)

## ## 🔍 Business Questions

1. Which product categories and sub-categories generate the highest sales and profit?
2. Which regions contribute the most to overall sales and profitability?
3. How do discounts affect profit margins across products?
4. What monthly and seasonal trends can be observed in sales performance?
5. Which products generate high sales but low or negative profits?
6. Which customer segments contribute the most to total revenue?

## 💡 Key Findings

- **Sales vs. Profitability:** High sales do not always translate into high profits; product-level profit analysis is important for evaluating performance.
- **Regional Performance:** Sales and profitability can vary across regions, highlighting opportunities to investigate underperforming markets.
- **Discount Impact:** Heavy discounting may reduce profit margins, particularly for products that already have low profitability.
- **Product Performance:** Some sub-categories may generate strong revenue but weak or negative profit, requiring closer review of pricing and costs.
- **Sales Trends:** Monthly sales analysis can help identify seasonal demand patterns and support inventory planning.
- **Customer Segments:** Comparing customer segments by sales and profit can help prioritize retention and marketing activities.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- **Optimize Discount Strategies:** Review discount levels for low-margin products and evaluate whether promotions improve overall profitability.
- **Improve Product Profitability:** Investigate products with negative profit and review pricing, costs, and discount policies.
- **Strengthen Regional Strategy:** Analyze high-performing regions to identify successful practices and investigate the causes of weaker performance elsewhere.
- **Plan for Seasonal Demand:** Use monthly sales trends to support inventory management, staffing, and promotional planning.
- **Focus on Valuable Customers:** Identify customer segments that contribute strongly to both revenue and profit, then develop targeted retention campaigns.
- **Monitor Business KPIs:** Track sales, profit, profit margin, and order volume together rather than relying on revenue alone.
